Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Iron Curtain Racing ("the game") is made by Unblocked Games (Attila Dán, Hungary; "we"). This policy explains what data the game handles, why, and what choices you have. Questions and requests: ironcurtain.developer@gmail.com.

What the game itself keeps

The game has no account and no server of its own. Your progress (money, cars, paint, stickers, settings, missions), your answer to the ads question and the photos you take in photo mode are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you delete the game. A photo leaves your device only if you share it yourself through your phone's share sheet.

Ads

The game shows ads between races and offers optional rewarded ads. They are served through Unity LevelPlay, with its ironSource Ads and Unity Ads networks, operated by Unity Technologies and its affiliates. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these services collect from your device: the device's advertising identifier and other device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information (model, operating system, language, connection type), how you interact with the ads, and diagnostic data about the ads' performance.

When you first open the garage, the game asks whether the ad networks may use your advertising identifier to show you personalised ads. If you allow it, the ads may be personalised. If you decline, you still see ads, but without personalisation based on your data. You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy. On iPhone and iPad, if you allow personalised ads, iOS also asks you separately whether the game may track you (App Tracking Transparency); you can change that answer in the iOS Settings. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device's settings.

Unity's privacy policy describes how it handles this data: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The game is told what was bought, so it can give you the item and restore your purchases.

Leaderboards and achievements

If you sign in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS), the game sends your best lap times and the achievements you earn to that service, under your player profile there. Google and Apple handle this data under their own privacy policies (policies.google.com/privacy, apple.com/legal/privacy). Google Play Games also collects diagnostic and analytics data about its own operation.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules them on your device itself (your car is repaired, today's challenge). There is no server behind them and no data leaves your device for them. You can switch them off at any time in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Legal bases (EEA, UK)

Your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings › Privacy.

Purchases

Leaderboards and achievements

Transfers

The ad, store and game services may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards they describe in their policies (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

How long

Data on your device stays until you delete the game. The services above keep data for the periods set out in their own policies.

Security

We hold no personal data about you on systems of our own. The services above state that they encrypt the data they collect while it is sent.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority (in Hungary, the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság, naih.hu). Since we hold no data about you on our own systems, requests about ad data are best sent to Unity, and about leaderboards and purchases to Google or Apple; we will help with any request sent to ironcurtain.developer@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
