Five-year plan for speed

Real cars.
Unreal ambitions.

The family cars your parents queued years for, now with a roll cage, wide tyres and an engine that was never in any official plan.

Build it in the garage

Every car starts on the lift in a backstreet garage. Tune it, paint it, make it yours.

  • Tune the engine on the dyno and watch the power curve move
  • Paint, stickers, race numbers and ready-made liveries
  • Tyres, helmets and lucky charms that swing from the mirror

Feel every kerb

Real car physics, with an Arcade mode for fun and a Simulation mode for purists. Drive from the chase cam, the bonnet or from behind the wheel.

  • Tilt, buttons or an on-screen steering wheel
  • Haptics that rumble over the kerbs
  • Cars that dent, scratch and crack their glass

Then the weather turns

Race the circuits in summer sun or pouring rain: spray off the wheels, drops on the glass and thunder over the grandstands. The hillclimb is always under snow.

Missions, goals and photos

Every track has its missions: beat the clock, win the sprint, hit the targets. Fifty-four goals, with leaderboards on Game Center and Google Play Games.

  • A daily challenge and a gift every day
  • Photo mode on the track and in the garage
